# Third-party and research notice

QuixSEO's original source is MIT. Reference repositories were inspected for
features and verified behavior; their code, branding and proprietary assets were
not copied. Yoast is GPLv3 and Rank Math GPLv3+; neither is a dependency. The
Rank Math analyzer was inspected, not reused. The jdevalk EmDash plugin is MIT
but its source was also not reused.

The runtime bundle uses Zod (MIT). EmDash and its Block Kit/types/official CLI
and test tools are separate platform/development dependencies under their
respective licenses. Preserve dependency notices when redistributing bundled
third-party code. package-lock.json records exact resolved packages. Review
dependency notices and the release archive before publication.
